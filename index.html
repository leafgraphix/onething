The Studio

We design the dream,
not just the render.

Leaf Graphix is a Kerala-based concept design studio — not a render house. We work in the space between a client's vision and the architect's first drawing, giving shape to ideas through AI-assisted concept art, mood boards and brand direction.

Every villa, ornament or identity we touch carries a point of view rooted in Kerala's own materials, light and craft — dressed in a cinematic, dark-gold language that feels premium without losing warmth.
